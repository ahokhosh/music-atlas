About Music Atlas

Music Atlas is the music engine of the BidBes ecosystem — a free window onto the world's music, powered by Music Atlas. Search millions of full-length tracks, ride the trending charts of every nation, and build personal playlists without a subscription or a paywall in sight.

What you get

  • Full-length streaming — complete tracks (progressive MP3 + HLS), not 30-second previews
  • Per-country charts — trending music for every ISO 3166-1 country
  • Genre packs — Top, Pop, Hip-Hop, Electronic, Lofi, Persian, K-Pop, Afrobeats and more
  • Playlist import — paste any track or playlist link to play or save it
  • Personal library — cloud playlists synced to your BidBes identity

Who it is for

Music explorers mapping the sound of other countries, producers sampling global scenes, expats chasing home-market hits, and anyone tired of paywalls. Free, no account required to listen — sign in only to keep playlists.
